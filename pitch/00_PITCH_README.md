## ⚠️ Golden Rules Before You Walk In

1. **Speaker 1 must cry internally, not externally** — controlled emotion, not a breakdown.
2. **Speaker 2 must never say "we think" or "we hope"** — say "we have", "we built", "it works".
3. **Speaker 3 (you) must slow down** — technical confidence is communicated by pace, not speed.
4. **Handoffs must be clean** — each speaker ends with a pass-line to the next.
5. **No one reads from slides** — know the script. Slides are props, not scripts.
6. **If challenged: pause, breathe, label** — "That is a fair concern. Here is how we addressed it."
