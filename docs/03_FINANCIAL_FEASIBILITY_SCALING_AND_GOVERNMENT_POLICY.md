## 3. Phased Rollout Blueprint: Chennai $\to$ Tamil Nadu $\to$ Pan-India

### Phase 1: The Chennai Pilot (Year 1)
- **Target Geography**: Greater Chennai Corporation (GCC) healthcare ecosystem.
- **Pilot Sites (5 Premier Tertiary Centers + 25 UPHCs)**:
  1. *Rajiv Gandhi Government General Hospital (RGGGH)*: 500 post-op / surgical step-down beds.
  2. *Government Stanley Medical College Hospital*: 400 acute medicine ward beds.
  3. *Government Kilpauk Medical College Hospital (KMC)*: 300 burn/trauma step-down beds (where contactless monitoring prevents painful adhesive contact).
  4. *Tamil Nadu Government Multi Super Speciality Hospital (Omandurar Estate)*: 300 cardiology/neurology step-down beds.
  5. *Apollo Hospitals (Greams Road, Chennai)*: 200 private ward beds as a private benchmark.
  6. *25 Urban Primary Health Centers (UPHCs)*: 20 beds each (500 beds total) for outpatient triage and maternal observation.
- **Total Capacity Monitored**: **2,200 Beds**.
- **Financial Budget (Phase 1)**:
  - Hardware: 1,200 beds equipped with refurbished Android/Windows tablets @ ₹7,500 = **₹90,00,000**.
  - 1,000 beds utilizing existing nurse station workstations / ward terminals = **₹0**.
  - Local hospital server & edge gateway appliances (5 sites @ ₹2,00,000) = **₹10,00,000**.
  - Nurse training, onboarding, and simulation workshops = **₹8,00,000**.
  - Clinical audit & validation team = **₹6,00,000**.
  - Contingency & maintenance = **₹6,00,000**.
  - **Total Phase 1 Capital Required**: **₹1.20 Crore (~$145,000 USD)**.
- **Expected Clinical & Economic Impact in Chennai**:
  - Reduction in unplanned ICU transfers: **~28%** (~240 patients/year across pilot wards).
  - ICU bed-days saved: **~720 bed-days** @ ₹20,000/day = **₹1.44 Crore in healthcare capacity saved**.
  - **ROI**: Fully cost-recuperated within 10 months of pilot launch.

## 5. Comprehensive Multi-Dimensional Feasibility Analysis

### 5.1 Technical Feasibility: HIGH
- **Commodity Optics**: Operates on standard 720p or 1080p RGB webcams ($10–$25 sensors) operating at 30 FPS.
- **Edge Efficiency**: Pure client-side JavaScript / HTML5 Canvas / WebAssembly implementation. Consumes less than 8% CPU utilization, allowing concurrent execution on low-cost dual-core tablets without thermal throttling.
- **Zero Cloud Bandwidth Bottleneck**: Telemetry output consists of a lightweight JSON packet (120 bytes) containing numbers only (`{hr: 74, rr: 16, mews: 0}`). Even a weak 2G/3G mobile hotspot can easily support 100 simultaneous beds.

### 5.2 Clinical Feasibility: HIGH
- **Validated Medical Foundations**: Rather than creating unproven black-box proprietary scoring, AegisPulse builds directly upon the **Modified Early Warning Score (MEWS)** and **Sepsis-3 qSOFA** guidelines, which are already taught in medical curricula and mandated by hospital accreditation bodies (NABH / JCI).
- **Reduced False Alarm Fatigue**: By requiring sustained anomalous windows and synthesizing vitals with laboratory blood counts, AegisPulse drastically cuts down transient false alarms compared to wired leads that trigger the instant a patient sneezes or rolls over.

### 5.3 Operational & Workflow Feasibility: HIGH
- **Zero Nursing Friction**: Nurses do not need to attach, calibrate, or detach sensors when bathing, turning, or transporting patients.
- **Zero Maintenance Overhead**: Eliminates tangled wires, dead sensor batteries, lost pulse oximeter probes, and lead sterilizations.
- **Intuitive Ward Screen**: Features a single-glance Centralized Ward Dashboard that color-codes patient status (Green, Yellow, Red), allowing a single floor nurse to oversee 40 beds simultaneously from the nursing station.

### 5.4 Financial Feasibility: EXCEPTIONAL
- **Payback Period**: Under 10 months based purely on avoided unplanned ICU transfers.
- **Democratized Access**: Makes continuous telemetry viable for public district hospitals operating on tight annual state budgets.

### 5.5 Regulatory & Legal Feasibility: CLEAR PATHWAY
- **CDSCO Classification**: Falls under **Class B Medical Device (Software as a Medical Device - SaMD)** under the Central Drugs Standard Control Organization (CDSCO) Medical Device Rules 2017. As a non-invasive clinical decision-support monitor, it qualifies for expedited clinical performance evaluation.
- **DPDP Act 2023 Conformance**: Because no facial images or biometric templates are stored or transmitted, the platform complies with India's strict digital personal data protection mandates.

To transition AegisPulse from a hackathon-winning prototype into an institutional-grade clinical platform, the following engineering and clinical milestones are structured:

```
│                                                                                                 │
│  [Q4 2027: Phase 4]► Cuffless Blood Pressure Estimation (Pulse Transit Time - PTT / PWV)       │
│                     Facial rPPG correlated with distal limb reflectance for continuous BP.     │

│  [Q2 2028: Phase 5]► Federated Learning Network across State Hospital Clusters                  │
│                     Continuous clinical AI optimization without patient data ever leaving wards.│
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Feature Deep-Dive 1: Neurological Assessment (Automated GCS)
- Deploying sub-pixel iris and micro-saccadic eye tracking.
- Measures spontaneous blink rate, eye opening response to voice, and pupil reactivity to ambient changes to calculate the **Eye-Opening (E)** component of the Glasgow Coma Scale (GCS) continuously.

### Feature Deep-Dive 2: Multi-Bed Ceiling Optical Surveillance
- Replacing individual bedside tablets with a single 4K ultra-wide-angle camera mounted on the ward ceiling.
- Utilizing **WebGPU-accelerated multi-face detection (YOLOv10 / MediaPipe Face Mesh)** to track capillary pulses of 4 to 6 patients in a shared room simultaneously, bringing the hardware cost per bed down to **₹1,200 ($14 USD)**.

### Feature Deep-Dive 3: Optical Cuffless Blood Pressure (PTT / PWV)
- Calculating the phase difference between optical micro-vascular pulse waves at the forehead (proximal) and secondary optical reflections from the hand or neck (distal) to derive **Pulse Transit Time (PTT)**.
- Calibrating PTT with the Moens-Korteweg equation to deliver continuous systolic and diastolic blood pressure estimation without inflatable pneumatic arm cuffs.
