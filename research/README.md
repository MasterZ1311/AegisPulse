# AegisPulse Research & Benchmarking Dossier

This directory houses research datasets, statistical benchmark scripts, and literature review records supporting the AegisPulse Patient Deterioration Radar.

## Contents & References
- **Bland-Altman Validation**: Empirical accuracy tests against Contec CMS50D finger pulse oximeter across Fitzpatrick skin phototypes I–VI.
- **POS Algorithm Foundations**: Plane-Orthogonal-to-Skin (Wang et al., IEEE TBME 2017) chrominance projection methodology.
- **Clinical Early Warning Standards**: Modified Early Warning Score (MEWS, Subbe et al., 2001) and Sepsis-3 (qSOFA, Seymour et al., 2016).
- **Full Research Paper**: See [docs/01_RESEARCH_PAPER_EXTENDED_AND_STATISTICAL_BENCHMARKS.md](../docs/01_RESEARCH_PAPER_EXTENDED_AND_STATISTICAL_BENCHMARKS.md).
